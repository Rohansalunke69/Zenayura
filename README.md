# 🌿 Zenayura – AI Powered Ayurvedic Health Platform

Zenayura is an AI-powered Ayurvedic health platform designed to help users connect with verified Ayurvedic doctors in Nagpur and receive guidance for natural healthcare.

The platform combines traditional Ayurvedic knowledge with modern technology to make authentic Ayurvedic consultation accessible online.

---

# 🩺 About the Platform

Zenayura is built to solve a real problem:  
Many people want to follow Ayurvedic treatment but struggle to find trusted and verified doctors.

This platform allows users to:

• Discover verified Ayurvedic doctors from Nagpur  
• Connect with doctors and book appointments  
• Use an AI health assistant for preliminary health guidance  
• Manage personal health profiles

⚠️ All doctors listed on the platform are real Ayurvedic practitioners.

---

# 👨‍⚕️ Verified Doctors from Nagpur

Zenayura features Ayurvedic doctors from Nagpur who are verified before being listed on the platform.

Users can:

• Browse available doctors  
• View doctor profiles  
• Book appointments  
• Connect for consultation

The goal is to make trusted Ayurvedic care easily accessible online.

---

# 🤝 Built with Doctor Guidance

Zenayura is developed under the guidance of Ayurvedic doctors to ensure that the platform reflects authentic Ayurvedic principles.

This helps maintain:

• credibility  
• trust  
• accurate representation of Ayurvedic practices

---

# 🤖 AI Health Assistant

Zenayura also includes an AI-powered Ayurvedic health assistant.

Users can describe symptoms and receive:

• basic Ayurvedic insights  
• lifestyle suggestions  
• general herbal recommendations

⚠️ The AI assistant provides informational guidance only and does not replace professional medical consultation.
